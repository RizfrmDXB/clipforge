ClipForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ClipForge is a desktop application operated by the GTARizzclips creator. It runs entirely on the operator’s own computer and helps edit gaming clips into vertical short videos and publish them to the operator’s own connected social accounts (TikTok, YouTube).

What data ClipForge handles

  • Account tokens. When the operator connects a TikTok or YouTube account through the platform’s official login, ClipForge receives an access token. Tokens are stored encrypted on the operator’s computer and are used only to upload videos and read basic profile and post statistics for the connected account.
  • Basic profile information. Display name and avatar of the connected account, used only to label the account inside the app.
  • Video content and statistics. Videos created by the operator, and view or engagement counts of the operator’s own posts, where the platform’s API provides them.

What ClipForge does not do

  • It does not collect data about anyone other than the operator’s own connected accounts.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer data to third parties.
  • It does not use tracking, advertising, or analytics services.
  • It does not store data on remote servers; everything stays on the operator’s computer.

Data retention and deletion

All data lives in local files under the operator’s control and can be deleted at any time by disconnecting the account in the app or deleting the app’s data folder. Disconnecting an account deletes its stored token. Platform access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Google account security settings.
