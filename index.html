The AI clip studio behind GTARizzclips

ClipForge is a desktop application that watches authorized gaming livestreams, detects the moments viewers go wild for, and edits them into captioned vertical shorts. Finished clips are published to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube channels.

⚡ Viral moment detection

Chat-spike and audio-excitement analysis finds the exact seconds a stream explodes, with no scrubbing through VODs.

✂️ Automatic editing

Vertical 1080×1920 at 60fps, dead-air jump cuts, word-synced captions, profanity filtering, and channel branding, all rendered automatically.

🎙 AI narration

Daily "Top 5" countdowns scripted from real detected moments and narrated with natural neural voices.

🔥 Trend radar

Tracks the most-viewed clips in the game right now and matches hot themes against the creator's own footage.

📝 Smart metadata

Specific, non-generic hooks, titles, captions and hashtags written from each clip's actual transcript.

📅 Scheduled publishing

Approved clips are queued and posted to the creator's TikTok and YouTube accounts with spacing and daily caps.

How it works

  1. Monitor: the app watches livestreams the creator has permission to clip, reading public chat activity.
  2. Detect: spikes in chat and audio excitement mark highlight moments in real time.
  3. Edit: each moment is cut, tightened, captioned and branded into a ready-to-post vertical short.
  4. Review: a human reviews every clip and approves or rejects it. Nothing posts without approval.
  5. Publish: approved clips go out to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube channels on a schedule.
Content rights. ClipForge only records and clips streams from creators who have given the GTARizzclips operator permission to reuse their content. It is a single-operator tool for the creator's own social accounts. It does not host, redistribute or sell third-party content.
